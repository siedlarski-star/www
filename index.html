Why LingoBar

Better than flashcards.
Better than any app.

1

Always there, never annoying

No notifications, no pop-ups, no streaks to maintain. Just a new word every few minutes, right where you look dozens of times a day. Learning happens in the background — effortlessly.

2

Active recall — the most effective technique

See the word in the foreign language, try to recall the meaning, then click to confirm. This is how memory actually works — you're not passively reading, you're actively building vocabulary. Example sentences show each word in real context. German articles and plurals, Spanish gender — all the details that matter.

3

You stay in control

Change words every 1, 5, 10, 30, 60 or 90 minutes. Hide words you already know. Save favorites. Add your own vocabulary. Track how many words you've mastered per language pair. Your learning, your rules.

Features

Everything you need.

⌨️

Pure menu bar

No Dock icon, no windows cluttering your desktop. Hold ⌘ and drag to place it wherever you want. Launches automatically when you log in.

🌍

Any language pair

12 languages: Spanish, English, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Swedish, Hungarian, Russian, Arabic and Korean. Pick what you speak and what you want to learn. Switch anytime.

✎

My Words

Add your own vocabulary — words from class, a textbook, or anything you want to drill. Up to 200 personal words in rotation alongside the built-in categories.

📊

Dashboard & progress

One place to manage everything: see how many words you know, browse all 1,500+ words across 23 categories, hide the ones you've mastered and fine-tune how often each appears. Progress tracked separately for every language pair.

★

Favorites & browsing

Star any word to save it to your favorites list. Browse the full word list for any category, search and filter — then decide exactly which words stay in rotation.

💬

Example sentences

Every word comes with a real example sentence in both languages — so you see it in context, not just as an isolated translation. Hear the sentence read aloud too.

🇩🇪

Articles & plurals where they matter

German words show the article (der/die/das) and the plural form. Spanish words show gender in brackets. The details that actually make you fluent.

🔊

Hear every word & sentence

Tap to hear the word and its example sentence read aloud. Pick your favorite system voice — and the higher-quality natural voices are supported too.

🔒

Private by design

Zero data collection. No account, no tracking. Everything stays on your Mac, with optional iCloud sync across your own devices. And zero ads — not in the free version, not in premium. Ever.
