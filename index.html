In action

See it for yourself

Native macOS app. No Dock icon. Hold ⌘ and drag to place it anywhere in your menu bar.

Always in your menu bar

A word in the language you're learning, right at the top of your screen all day. Show translation next to it — or keep it hidden for a daily challenge.

Click to reveal & listen

Left-click to see the full translation in a popover. Hit the sound button to hear the word pronounced. Like a mini quiz, every time. Closes automatically after 5 seconds.

20 categories, 2 levels

Beginner and Advanced, 20 categories — including DELE A1/A2 and B1/B2 vocabulary. Words in brackets remind you of gender and form.

Your language pair & pace

Choose what you speak and what you want to learn. Set how often words change — every 1, 5, 10, 30, 60 or 90 minutes. Adapts to your day.

My Words

Add your own vocabulary — words from class, a textbook, or anything you want to practice. Up to 200 personal words, always in rotation.

Favorites & progress

Star words you want to master. Mark words you already know to skip them. Watch your 🏆 count grow — tracked separately for every language pair.

Why LingoBar

Better than flashcards.
Better than any app.

1

Always there, never annoying

No notifications, no pop-ups, no streaks to maintain. Just a new word every few minutes, right where you look dozens of times a day. Learning happens in the background — effortlessly.

2

Active recall — the most effective technique

See the word in the foreign language, try to recall the meaning, then click to confirm. This is how memory actually works — you're not passively reading, you're actively building vocabulary. Words in brackets help you remember gender and context.

3

You stay in control

Change words every 1, 5, 10, 30, 60 or 90 minutes. Hide words you already know. Save favorites. Add your own vocabulary. Track how many words you've mastered per language pair. Your learning, your rules.

Features

Everything you need.

⌨️

Pure menu bar

No Dock icon, no windows cluttering your desktop. Hold ⌘ and drag to place it wherever you want. Launches automatically when you log in.

🌍

Any language pair

7 languages: Spanish, English, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, Polish. Pick what you speak and what you want to learn. Switch the pair anytime from the menu.

Mark words you already know and watch your count grow. Tracked separately for every language pair — so switching from Spanish to French doesn't reset your progress.

Private by design

Zero data collection. No account, no cloud, no tracking. Everything stays on your Mac. And zero ads — not in the free version, not in premium. Ever.
